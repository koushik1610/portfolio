# Yahoo IC Leveling Criteria

## INDIVIDUAL CONTRIBUTOR (IC1–IC4)

| CRITERIA | IC1 — Entry Professional | IC2 — Developing Professional | IC3 — Career Professional | IC4 — Sr. Professional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **OVERALL POSITION SCOPE AND ACCOUNTABILITY (INCLUDING FUNCTION CONTRIBUTION)** | Performs routine assignments by following existing procedures. Receives instruction, guidance and direction from more senior-level roles. Has impact on own work team. | Performs a range of assignments related to job discipline. Uses prescribed guidelines or policies in analyzing situations. Receives a moderate level of guidance and direction. Impacts the quality of work and the work of others on the team. | Works independently with minimal guidance and direction. May lead small project teams or project phases/workstreams of larger scope. Acts as a resource for colleagues with less experience. Impacts a range of customer, operational, project or service activities within own team and related work teams. | Works independently with guidance only in complex situations. May lead functional teams or projects with moderate resource requirements, risks and/or complexity. May lead others to solve complex work problems, or network with key contacts outside own area. Impacts a range of customer, operational, project or service objectives. |
| **FUNCTIONAL KNOWLEDGE** | Requires conceptual knowledge of theories, practices and procedures in own job discipline. | Requires working knowledge and experience in own job discipline. Continues to build professional expertise, including knowledge of the company, processes, customers, etc. | Requires in-depth conceptual and practical knowledge in own job discipline, and basic knowledge of related job disciplines. | Requires specialized depth and/or breadth of expertise in own job discipline or field. |
| **PROBLEM SOLVING & CRITICAL THINKING** | Follows established policies and procedures to solve standard or routine problems. Analyzes information and standard practices to make judgments. | Solves a variety of problems of moderate scope and within own area. May work on a range of assignments that are semi-routine in nature but related to job discipline. Analyzes possible solutions using technical experience and judgment, but typically within defined practices and procedures. Recognizes the need for occasional deviation from accepted practices. | Develops solutions to a variety of moderately complex or unusual problems in own area. Applies independent judgement to develop solutions or make recommendations based on the analysis of a variety of factors or information sources. Demonstrates the ability to take a new perspective on existing solutions. | Develops solutions to a variety of complex and multifaceted problems in own area. Uses sophisticated analytical thought to exercise judgment and identify innovative solutions. |

## INDIVIDUAL CONTRIBUTOR (IC5–IC9)

| CRITERIA | IC5 — Advanced Professional | IC6 — Director | IC7 — Senior Director (Selective Technical Track Only) | IC8 — VP I (Selective Technical Track Only) | IC9 — VP II (Selective Technical Track Only) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **OVERALL POSITION SCOPE AND ACCOUNTABILITY (INCLUDING FUNCTION CONTRIBUTION)** | Works independently with guidance only in the most complex situations. May lead functional teams or projects with complex resource requirements, risks and challenges. Impacts the achievement of customer, operational, project or service objectives across work teams. | Contributes to the development of organization functional strategy. Leads projects with notable risk and complexity; develops the strategy for project execution. Impacts the direction and resource allocation for programs, projects or services. | May participate in the development of new products, services or business strategy. Leads highly visible multidisciplinary project teams or initiatives; provides thought leadership. Impacts business direction through the development of innovative services or products. | Prime strategist for the most critical initiatives that impact the future success of the organization and/or industry. Leads the most highly visible multidisciplinary project teams or initiatives; provides thought leadership. Impacts strategic business direction through the development of the most innovative solutions. | Prime strategist for the most critical initiatives that impact the future success of the organization and/or industry. Leads the most highly visible multidisciplinary project teams or initiatives; provides thought leadership. Impacts strategic business direction through the development of the most innovative solutions. |
| **FUNCTIONAL KNOWLEDGE** | Requires highly specialized depth and/or breadth of expertise in own job discipline or field and practical knowledge of other job disciplines. | Regarded within the organization as a technical expert in their job discipline. Requires in-depth and/or breadth of expertise in own job discipline and broad knowledge of other job disciplines within the organization function. | Regarded within the organization as the "go-to" technical expert in their job discipline. Requires in-depth expertise in leading-edge theories, techniques and/or technologies. | Regarded externally as an industry "guru" or expert in a job discipline. Requires comprehensive breadth and depth of expertise in leading-edge theories, techniques and/or technologies. | Regarded externally as an industry "guru" or expert in a job discipline. Requires comprehensive breadth and depth of expertise in leading-edge theories, techniques and/or technologies. |
| **PROBLEM SOLVING & CRITICAL THINKING** | Develops solutions to a variety of complex and multifaceted problems in and/or across multiple areas. Uses sophisticated analytical thought to identify and develop highly innovative solutions. | Solves unique problems with broad impact on the business. Requires conceptual and innovative thinking to develop new and creative practices/solutions. | Proactively identifies and solves highly complex problems. Develops ground-breaking methods to think beyond existing solutions. | Proactively identifies and solves the most complex problems that impact the strategic direction of the business. Develops ground-breaking methods that shape the long-term course of the organization and/or industry. | Proactively identifies and solves the most complex problems that impact the strategic direction of the business. Develops ground-breaking methods that shape the long-term course of the organization and/or industry. |

## Key Transitions

### IC4 → IC5 (Current → Target)

| Dimension | IC4 (Current) | IC5 (Target) | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Moderate resource/risk/complexity projects | Complex resource/risk/challenge projects | From "moderate" to "complex" |
| **Independence** | Guidance in complex situations | Guidance only in most complex situations | Near-full autonomy |
| **Impact** | Customer/operational/project/service objectives | Objectives **across work teams** | Cross-team impact required |
| **Knowledge** | Specialized depth/breadth in own discipline | Highly specialized + practical knowledge of **other disciplines** | Must demonstrate breadth beyond cloud security |
| **Problem Solving** | Complex, multifaceted problems in own area | Complex, multifaceted problems **in and/or across multiple areas** + **highly innovative solutions** | Cross-domain problem solving + innovation |

### IC5 → IC6 (Stretch Target)

| Dimension | IC5 | IC6 | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Leads projects | Leads projects AND contributes to org strategy | Strategy contribution |
| **Impact** | Across work teams | Direction and resource allocation for programs | Influences org direction |
| **Knowledge** | Highly specialized | Regarded as technical expert in org | Recognized authority |
| **Problem Solving** | Innovative solutions | Unique problems with broad business impact | Business-level impact |
