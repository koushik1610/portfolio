# Project card image prompts

Witty oil-painting metaphors for each project card, in the Cassius Coolidge
"Dogs Playing Poker" style. The whole set shares one style spine so the cards
read as a gallery.

## How to use

Paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then one SCENE after it, into ChatGPT. One image
per request. Keep the STYLE BLOCK identical every time and only swap the scene,
or the look drifts between cards.

## STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every prompt)

> Warm, painterly oil-painting in the style of Cassius Coolidge's "Dogs Playing
> Poker" — rich shadows, cozy single-lamp lighting, characterful animals as the
> actors, humorous but tasteful. Subtle glowing neural circuitry and faint
> geometric nodes woven into the scene to signal AI. Landscape 16:9, muted
> palette with one warm accent. No text or logos in the image.

Add your theme accent at the end, e.g. "warm accent in deep teal."

## Scenes

### LLM Council / Autonomous Threat Intelligence Pipeline
A relay race of different dog breeds in a wood-paneled war room, each passing a
glowing baton down a line of desks labeled by station, a sharp-eyed conductor
dog routing the baton to whichever runner is fastest. Five distinct breeds =
five model providers.

### Artemis (AI-SPM platform)
A keen hunting hound in ranger's gear standing on a ridge at dusk, surveying a
vast valley where two distant fortress-cities (one blue, one red) are connected
by glowing trail-lines like constellations; the hound traces one bright path to
a treasure vault.

### Security Ops Platform
A wise owl in a dispatcher's chair at a control desk covered in small alert
lamps, one paw resting on a large brass "DENY" lever, calmly sorting glowing
tickets into trays.

### Antitoxin (IAM toxic combinations)
A composed dog in a vest holding shears, cutting one keystone strand of a
tangled glowing web of interlocked chains and snakes; the whole web visibly
begins to collapse from that single cut.

### review-aws-iam-policies (IAM audit agent)
A detective dog with a magnifying glass and deerstalker examining a tall wall of
hanging keys and locks, following a faintly glowing thread that links a few keys
into a hidden chain.

### AI Toolkit
A clever fox at a tidy workbench under a lamp, surrounded by many specialized,
well-organized custom tools; several small distinct animal figurines (the
personas) arranged in a half-circle as if in council.

### Security Review Workspace
A scholarly owl librarian in a vast archive of glowing floor-to-ceiling files, a
small brass robot assistant pulling exactly the right folder; faint node-lines
connect the shelves into one knowledge web.

### Baseline Research Pipeline
Three birds of ascending size (sparrow, hawk, eagle) on a moving conveyor, each
drafting and stamping documents at its own station; the smallest sorts fast, the
largest signs the finished baseline.

### Security Intelligence Notifier
A herald/postman dog in a cap sorting glowing envelopes into two bins marked by
simple icons (a check and an X), one paw reaching to ring a small bell.

### Data Ingestion POC (serverless Slack bot)
A beaver engineer in overalls at a wall of brass pipes and valves, funneling a
stream of glowing water-data through a clean serverless plumbing system into
labeled holding tanks.

### AWS Security Baselines
An architect dog in a hard hat reading large blueprints, directing the laying of
engraved foundation stones into a solid wall, each stone a control.

### ECS Signatures
A row of alert sentry dogs in matching uniforms standing watch along a long
stone wall at night, lanterns glowing, each scanning a different stretch of the
valley below.
